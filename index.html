Climate · type

Moderately continental

The country sits between an oceanic (maritime) climate in the west and a continental one in the east.

  • Influences: moist air masses from the Atlantic Ocean and the Baltic Sea
  • High humidity all year round
  • Moderate temperatures and fairly uniform precipitation
Atlantic and Baltic air softens winters and keeps the air damp, while the continental interior lets cold arrive in spells (Arctic air in winter) — so the climate is a blend rather than one pure type.

    Takeaways

    Climate shapes the forest

    1. A mild, humid, moderately continental climate between maritime and continental zones.
    2. Winters get colder and snowier towards the north-east, milder towards the south-west.
    3. Precipitation is 600–700 mm a year, about 70% of it in the warm months.
    4. Forests cover 40–43% of the land and keep growing; pine dominates, with spruce in the cold north and oak in the south.
    5. Warming is bringing milder winters and more thaws.
